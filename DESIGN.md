# UTKCODE — Design

## Creative direction
A **digital flex**. The website itself is the portfolio. 80% creative
studio, 20% sales machine. Editorial-meets-technical print language:
ink-black canvas, warm paper text, a single acid accent, oversized
compressed type, mono editorial metadata, and hairline rules.

## The feel
- **Editorial / print** — numbered fields, mono labels, rules, cropping.
- **Technical precision** — micro-interactions, magnetic CTAs, custom cursor.
- **Restrained motion** — clip-path / translate reveals. One excellent
  interaction, not twenty.

## Distinction
A screenshot of the hero (a 300px+ `UTKCODE` wordmark over a hairline grid,
with a single acid accent bar) is recognizable on its own.

## Visual language
- **Type**: Syne (display, extrabold, tight tracking) + Space Grotesk
  (body) + JetBrains Mono (editorial metadata).
- **Color**: `#0a0a0a` ink / `#f2efe6` paper / `#ebff00` acid accent.
- **Structure**: 12-col grid, editorial numbering, oversized numerals,
  uppercase mono eyebrows.
- **Motion**: reveal on scroll, magnetic CTA, custom cursor, scroll progress.

## Sections
1. **Hero** — the wordmark as a graphic object; "Websites worth remembering."
2. **Marquee** — capability ticker.
3. **Work** — an art-directed runway; each project a different composition.
4. **Services** — typography-led, not a sales pitch.
5. **Capabilities** — engineering proof, editorial audit list.
6. **Contact** — closing flex: "Your website could look better. Let's fix that."
7. **Footer** — minimal mono strip.

## Anti-patterns avoided
No glassmorphism, no gradients-with-glow, no floating cards, no 3D, no stock
photos, no AI illustrations, no fake clients/testimonials/awards/stats, no
typical 3-column card grid, no generic FAQ.
